NETWORK SECURITY AND CLASSICAL CRYPTOGRAPHY MINI-PROJECT (2026-27)
Course: Network Security
Branch: Final Year ECM

Classical ciphers form the historical bedrock of contemporary cryptography.
By investigating monoalphabetic substitution, polyalphabetic substitution,
polygraphic ciphers, and transposition ciphers, students comprehend the
core concepts of confusion, diffusion, and cryptanalysis.
